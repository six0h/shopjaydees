# Seasonal Playbook

Outreach themes shift through the year to match when each segment buys apparel. From the pitch deck; the 2026-05-20 design spec carries the same calendar and adds segment weighting (schools heavy late August through February, teams heavy spring and fall, trades heavier spring and fall).

| Period | Segment focus | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March to May | Teams, trades | Get ready for the season |
| June to July | Schools, businesses | Lock in your fall order early |
| August to September | Schools, teams | Back to school, back to the field |
| October to November | Businesses | Year-end gear and appreciation |
| December to February | Schools, businesses | New year, fresh look |

Seasonal campaign adjustments and template updates are covered by the monthly management fee, and seasonal strategy shifts are planned in the monthly review sessions.
