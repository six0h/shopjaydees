# Seasonal Playbook

Outreach themes shift through the year to match when each segment buys apparel. **Revised 2026-07-22** to BC Lower Mainland buying occasions and custom-apparel lead time (~6-10 weeks cold-lead to delivered goods: reply, quote, art, proof, then 2-4 weeks production). The earlier calendar used round-number month boundaries and was never wired into the pipeline; this one is.

**Schools are out of scope for this outreach** (client decision, Jenn, 2026-07-22 — she runs school outreach herself). Segment focus below is businesses, teams, and trades only.

Each period sells the season that is *coming*, because lead time means an order placed today is delivered ~2 months out. The **selling season** word is the one term Ellie may use; naming any other season fails validation (see the [outreach messaging framework](../systems/outreach-messaging-framework.md) and the personalization guardrails spec).

| Period | Selling season | Theme | Segment focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| June to August | fall | Lock in your fall order early | Fall-sports teams, businesses |
| September to November | winter / holiday | Year-end gear and appreciation | Businesses, teams |
| December to February | spring | New year, fresh look | Businesses, spring-sports teams |
| March to May | summer | Gear up for your season | Teams, trades, businesses |

Anchors: BC school year starts the day after Labour Day (Sep 8 in 2026) and ends late June; fall outdoor leagues (BCCSL soccer, minor hockey) run Sep-March and register in spring/summer; spring/summer leagues (baseball, softball, spring soccer) run April-July; corporate stacks onto year-end and holiday appreciation. The one substantive shift from the old calendar: **September pivots to year-end/holiday rather than back-to-school**, because Sept-start apparel must be ordered by mid-August to produce in time, so September outreach is already too late for the fall occasion.

Seasonal campaign adjustments and template updates are covered by the monthly management fee, and seasonal strategy shifts are planned in the monthly review sessions.
