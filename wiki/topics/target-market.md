# Target Market

Who the [lead generation system](../systems/lead-generation-system.md) targets for Jaydees Apparel.

## Segments

Three prospect segments, each with its own templates and seasonal variation. The 2026-05-20 design spec breaks each into categories with discovery filters (industry, geography, company size):

- **Businesses** (year-round, heavier spring and fall for trades). Trades and contractors (size 5 to 200), restaurants and hospitality (5 to 100), fitness and wellness (5 to 50), real estate and property management (5 to 100), auto and trades shops (5 to 50). Product angle: branded work wear, safety vests, staff uniforms, branded merch, team polos and jackets.
- **Schools** (heavy late August through February). Elementary and secondary (size 10 to 500), independent and private schools (10 to 200, higher budgets), daycares and preschools (5 to 50), post-secondary clubs. Product angle: spirit wear, staff shirts, event merch, grad gear.
- **Teams** (heavy spring and fall). Youth sports leagues (size 5 to 100), adult rec leagues (5 to 50), dance and performance (5 to 50), community sport organizations (10 to 200). Product angle: jerseys, warmups, fan gear, tournament gear, performance wear.

## Geography

The pitch lays out a start-local, scale-wide expansion in phases:

- **Phase 1.** Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Mission, Maple Ridge (the Fraser Valley core; the contracted targeting configuration).
- **Phase 2.** Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Pitt Meadows.
- **Phase 3.** Richmond, Delta, North Vancouver, Vancouver proper.
- **Future.** Rest of BC, then nationwide.

Timing of seasonal outreach per segment lives in the [seasonal playbook](seasonal-playbook.md).
