# Wear It Forward

Jaydees Apparel's community giving program and the core differentiator in outreach messaging. As described in the messaging framework's sample emails: a portion of every order goes back to the buyer's school, team, or a community cause they choose.

## How it is used in outreach

- Positioned as the second beat of every message: a genuine reason to choose Jaydees, never the headline.
- For schools: builds school pride and raises funds at the same time.
- For teams: supporters order directly during a set window and a portion of every sale goes back to the team, no inventory, no hassle. Teams have raised thousands this way per the framework deck.
- For businesses: a simple way to look sharp and give back at the same time.
- Prospect research looks for community signals (sponsorships, charity work, supported causes) as natural bridges to the program.
