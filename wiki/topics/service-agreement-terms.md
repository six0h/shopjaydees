# Service Agreement Terms

Summary of the service agreement between SixOhQuad Consulting Inc. (provider, contact Cody Halovich, CEO, cody@sixohquad.com) and Jaydees Apparel (client, signatory Jennifer Milne). Engagement: lead generation system design, build, and ongoing management.

## Fees

- **Setup fee: $3,000.00 CAD plus GST (5 percent), $3,150.00 CAD total.** Split into a $1,500.00 down payment due on signing and a $1,500.00 final payment due on delivery. Delivery means the System is deployed, operational, and the client walkthrough is complete.
- **Monthly management: $300.00 CAD plus GST, $315.00 CAD total per month**, starting on the delivery date. Covers monitoring, maintenance, optimization, targeting adjustments, monthly review sessions, seasonal campaign and template updates, and support.
- Monthly invoices issue on the delivery date anniversary, due within fourteen days. Invoices unpaid after thirty days can suspend management services and pause automated operations.
- The down payment is non-refundable once work begins. If the client terminates before delivery, completed work is handed over and no further payments are owed.

## Term

- Minimum commitment of six months of monthly management from delivery, then month to month.
- After the initial term, either party can cancel the management component with thirty days written notice.
- On termination the client keeps full ownership of the System, code, configurations, and data. SixOhQuad provides up to fourteen days of transition support.

## Third-party costs

The client pays for all third-party subscriptions, estimated at 32 to 129 US dollars per month depending on usage; see [Third-party stack](third-party-stack.md).

## Other notable terms

- **Intellectual property.** On full payment of the setup fee the client owns the custom Work Product. SixOhQuad keeps pre-existing tools and methods, licensed to the client perpetually and non-exclusively. SixOhQuad holds portfolio rights to reference the project.
- **No guaranteed results.** The agreement disclaims any specific lead counts, response rates, or revenue outcomes. Liability is capped at fees paid in the preceding twelve months, with indirect damages excluded.
- **Compliance.** Outreach is subject to Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). SixOhQuad configures reasonable compliance measures (unsubscribe mechanisms, sending limits); the client holds ultimate compliance responsibility.
- **Governing law.** British Columbia, Canada. Disputes go through thirty days of good-faith negotiation first.
