# Tamara

A staff member at Jaydees Apparel, referenced by Jenn on the 2026-07-15 walkthrough. Only the details below are known; the transcript did not cover her role beyond this step.

## Role in the workflow

Owns the downstream **quote / opportunity intake** step: once a prospect becomes a live lead (handed over by the [Prospect handoff to CRM agent](../systems/prospect-crm-handoff-agent.md)), Tamara collects the quote/opportunity information correctly in the CRM.

Jenn described this as "her domain… hopefully not forever," and floated eventually training a ClickUp AI agent to take over the step. That is an aspirational aside, not a commitment — flagged because automating a person's role has people-impact worth handling deliberately.
