# Decision: LinkedIn Stays Manual

The agent writes LinkedIn connection requests and messages; the owner sends them by hand. Consistent across the design spec, pitch, and messaging framework.

## Rationale

LinkedIn aggressively flags automated outreach. Manual sending keeps the owner's account safe and adds a human touch. Targets per the design spec: twenty to forty connection requests per week with a 25 to 40 percent acceptance rate.
