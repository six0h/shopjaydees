# Decision: Start Small Then Scale

Outreach volume ramps up in stages instead of launching at full volume. Presented to the client in the implementation roadmap as "quality first".

## The ramp

1. **Test batch.** Five to ten sends to validate messaging and deliverability.
2. **Review together.** SixOhQuad and the client check open rates, reply rates, and messaging quality.
3. **Scale up.** Volume increases once targeting and templates are dialed in.

## Rationale

Blasting one hundred emails on day one risks deliverability damage and wasted prospects before the messaging is proven. A small validated batch protects the warmed domains and lets template fixes happen cheaply.
