The Sequence

Three touches.
Nine days.

Each touch adds value and builds the relationship. No repeated pitches. No pressure. After Touch 3 with no response, the lead cools off for 90 days.

1

Day 0 — Intro

Personalized value prop + Wear It Forward + soft CTA

2

Day 4 — Value

Share something useful — a specific idea for their situation, no hard sell

3

Day 9 — Open Door

Lighter tone, circle back with a different angle, genuinely leave the door open

Beyond Email

LinkedIn &
follow-ups.

LinkedIn Request

Short, personal, not a pitch. Just a friendly connection referencing something specific about their organization. You send these manually — keeps it human.

Touch 2 (Day 4)

Share something genuinely useful — a specific idea for their situation, like how similar schools use spirit wear as ongoing fundraisers, or how consistent branded gear helps at job sites.

Touch 3 (Day 9)

Brief, friendly check-in. Different angle, lighter tone. "If the timing isn't right, no worries — happy to connect whenever it makes sense." Genuinely leave the door open.
