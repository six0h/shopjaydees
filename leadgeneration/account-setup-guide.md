# Account Setup Guide — ShopJaydees Lead Generation

This guide walks you through setting up the accounts needed for your lead generation pipeline. Each service plays a specific role in finding, qualifying, and reaching prospects.

Once you've completed each step, Cody at SixOhQuad will handle all the technical configuration — connecting the tools, building the agents, and getting the pipeline running.

**Questions at any point?** Email cody@sixohquad.com.

---

## 1. ClickUp

**What it does:** Your central hub — every lead, draft message, and approval lives here. This is where you'll review and approve outreach each day.

**Cost:** Free plan to start. Paid plans available if you need more features later.

### Steps

1. Go to [clickup.com](https://clickup.com) and click **Get Started Free**
2. Sign up with your email or Google account
3. Create a new **Workspace** — name it something like "ShopJaydees"
4. Once your workspace is created, invite Cody:
   - Click your workspace name (bottom-left) → **Settings**
   - Go to **People** → **Invite**
   - Enter `cody@sixohquad.com` and set the role to **Admin**
   - Click **Send Invite**

---

## 2. LinkedIn Sales Navigator

**What it does:** Lets you search for and find target companies — businesses, schools, and teams in your area that could use custom apparel. You'll export company lists that feed into the rest of the pipeline.

**Cost:** Sales Navigator Core starts at ~$99 USD/month.

### Steps

1. Go to [linkedin.com/sales](https://www.linkedin.com/sales) and click **Start free trial** (or **Get Sales Navigator**)
2. Sign in with your LinkedIn account
3. Choose the **Sales Navigator Core** plan
4. That's it — Cody will walk you through how to use the search filters and export company lists when we start building the pipeline

No invite needed — you'll use Sales Navigator directly from your own LinkedIn account. Cody will provide search strategies and filter templates for each prospect segment.

---

## 3. cleanlists.ai

**What it does:** Takes the company lists you export from Sales Navigator and finds the right people to contact — decision-maker names, titles, verified email addresses, and LinkedIn profiles.

**Cost:** Varies by volume. Check [cleanlists.ai](https://cleanlists.ai) for current pricing.

### Steps

1. Go to [cleanlists.ai](https://cleanlists.ai) and sign up for an account
2. Choose a plan that fits your expected volume (Cody can advise on this)
3. Once your account is active, let Cody know so we can coordinate the enrichment workflow

---

## 4. Instantly

**What it does:** Sends your cold outreach emails, warms up your sending address so emails land in inboxes (not spam), and tracks opens and replies.

**Cost:** Starts at ~$30 USD/month for the Growth plan.

### Steps

1. Go to [instantly.ai](https://instantly.ai) and click **Start Free Trial**
2. Sign up with your email
3. Choose the **Growth** plan (you can start with the free trial to explore first)
4. Once your account is active, invite Cody:
   - Click the **gear icon** (Settings) in the left sidebar
   - Go to **Team**
   - Click **Invite Member**
   - Enter `cody@sixohquad.com`
   - Click **Send Invite**

### Important: Connect a Sending Email Address

You'll need a dedicated email address for cold outreach — don't use your main business email. We recommend creating a new email like `ellie@shopjaydees.com` or `hello@shopjaydees.com` specifically for outreach. Cody will help you connect and warm it up once your Instantly account is ready.

---

## 5. Google Cloud

**What it does:** Hosts the AI agents that run the pipeline and the Gemini AI that writes personalized messages. This is the engine behind the scenes.

**Cost:** Typically $2–10 USD/month total (AI usage + hosting).

### Steps

This one works a bit differently — Cody will create the Google Cloud project and set everything up. You just need to add your billing information so the (small) monthly charges go to your account.

1. Cody will send you an email invitation to join a Google Cloud project
2. Accept the invitation — you'll need a Google account (Gmail works, or any Google Workspace email)
3. Once you're in, add a billing method:
   - Go to [console.cloud.google.com/billing](https://console.cloud.google.com/billing)
   - Click **Link a billing account** (or **Create account** if prompted)
   - Add a credit card or payment method
   - Link it to the project Cody shared with you
4. That's it — Cody handles everything else in Google Cloud

If you run into any issues with the billing setup, just forward the screen or error to cody@sixohquad.com and we'll sort it out together.
