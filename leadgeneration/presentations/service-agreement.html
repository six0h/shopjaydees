1. Scope of Work

SixOhQuad Consulting Inc. (“SixOhQuad”) will design, build, and deploy an automated lead generation system (the “System”) for Jaydees Apparel (the “Client”). The System will perform the following functions:

  1. Prospect Discovery — Automated scraping of Google Maps and other sources to identify potential customers (businesses, schools, and sports teams) across configured geographies in BC’s Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley.
  2. Lead Enrichment — Automated research of discovered prospects including website scraping, decision-maker identification, email discovery, and lead quality scoring.
  3. Personalized Outreach — AI-generated multi-touch email sequences and LinkedIn connection request copy, tailored to each prospect using segment-specific templates.
  4. Outreach Delivery & Tracking — Sending approved emails through a cold email platform, tracking opens, replies, and engagement, and flagging warm leads for Client follow-up.
  5. Warm Lead Reply Handling — All cold outreach will be sent from separate domains (not the Client’s primary business domain) to protect the Client’s main domain reputation and deliverability. Prospect replies will be collected in the cold email platform’s unified inbox (Unibox). When a reply is detected, the System will automatically update the lead’s status in ClickUp to flag it for follow-up. The Client will review flagged leads during their daily ClickUp review, respond to warm prospects through the unified inbox, and transition established conversations to their primary business email as appropriate.

The System will be integrated with ClickUp for lead management, approval workflows, and reporting. All pipeline stages will run on scheduled automation with the Client responsible for daily review and approval of outreach drafts and warm lead follow-up (approximately 15–20 minutes per day).

2. Deliverables
  1. A fully functional lead generation agent deployed on Google Cloud, performing discovery, enrichment, personalization, and outreach across all configured segments and geographies.
  2. ClickUp workspace configuration with statuses, custom fields, and approval workflows.
  3. Cold email platform setup with domain warmup and campaign configuration.
  4. Targeting configuration for Phase 1 geographies (Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Mission, Maple Ridge) across business, school, and team segments.
  5. Segment-specific outreach templates with seasonal variation.
  6. A walkthrough session with the Client covering daily operations, the approval workflow, and system overview.
