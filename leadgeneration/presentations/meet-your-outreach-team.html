The big picture

An outreach team that runs itself

Think of the system as five specialists working a production line. A lead comes in one end as just a name on a list, and comes out the other end as a real person who's interested in ShopJaydees — sitting in front of you, ready for a conversation.

They run on a schedule, mostly early on weekday mornings, so fresh work is always waiting for you. You never have to start them, watch them, or understand how they work under the hood. In fact, across the whole system there are only two things that ever need you:

1

Point them at a target

Tell the team who to go after — for example, "breweries in Langley" — by picking from a few simple dropdowns. That's it. No lists to build, no research to do.

2

Reply to people who write back

Each day you spend a few minutes approving the outreach, then simply reply to the warm leads the team surfaces for you. You only ever talk to interested people.

Everything else is automatic. Finding companies, tracking down the right contact, researching each business, writing the emails, sending them on the right schedule, watching the inbox, and reviving quiet leads months later — all handled by the team, in the background.

Agent 1 · The Prospector

Finds the right businesses

Turns your targeting request into a list of real, contactable leads.

Runs  Weekday mornings You give it  A target (e.g. "restaurants in Abbotsford") It hands off  Scored leads

When you create a request like "Restaurants in Abbotsford — 25 leads," the Prospector goes out and finds real companies that match. For each one, it tracks down the right person to talk to — their name, their title, and a verified email address — so you're never emailing a generic info@ black hole.

It then gives every lead a fit score from 1 to 5, so the best-matched prospects rise to the top. And it checks each name against everyone you've already contacted, so nobody ever gets emailed twice.

  • Strong leads (scored 3 and up) move forward automatically to be written to.
  • Weaker leads are quietly set aside for later, not deleted.
  • You can override any score you disagree with.

Why it matters to you: you skip the most tedious part of outbound entirely — building lists and hunting for the right contact. You just say who you want, and a clean, prioritized list appears.

✍️

Agent 2 · The Copywriter

Writes to them in your voice

Turns a bare contact into ready-to-send, on-brand outreach.

Runs  Weekday mornings It receives  Strong leads from the Prospector It produces  A 3-email sequence + LinkedIn note

For each strong lead, the Copywriter visits the company's website, reads what they do, and looks for the human details — a sponsorship, a charity they support, a local event they run. Then it writes a warm, personalized set of three emails plus a short LinkedIn connection note.

Every message naturally works in your "Wear It Forward" community-giveback story and mentions something specific about that business — so it reads like a real note from you, not mass-produced spam. It's all signed by "Ellie," your consistent outreach persona. It also checks for any "do not contact" signals so everything stays compliant with Canada's anti-spam law.

Why it matters to you: you get outreach that sounds personal and on-brand for every single lead — the kind of writing that would take hours by hand — waiting for you to approve.

Your part

Your daily 15 minutes

This is the one recurring thing the system asks of you — and it's the important one, because it keeps you fully in control. Each morning, a fresh batch of personalized emails is waiting, marked "Ready for Review."

~15 min a day

Read a batch of pre-written emails and decide what goes out. Nothing is ever sent without your say-so.

For each one, you have three simple choices:

✅
ApproveLooks great — send it as written.
✏️
EditTweak a line, then approve.
🚫
SkipNot a fit — set it aside.

That's the whole job. Point the team at a target, then spend a few minutes a day approving outreach. Everything before and after this step runs on its own.

📬

Agent 3 · The Mailer

Sends, so it lands

Your mail carrier — delivers approved emails the right way.

Runs  Weekday mornings It receives  Only emails you approved It does  Delivers the 3-touch sequence

Once you've approved an email, the Mailer takes over and actually sends it — spacing the three touches out over time so the outreach feels natural, not pushy. It sends through a specialized cold-email service that's built to keep messages out of spam folders, and rotates across your dedicated outreach addresses to protect their reputation.

If an email address turns out to be dead, it's quietly flagged as bounced instead of being sent — so you're not wasting touches on bad contacts. Everything it does is recorded on the lead's card.

Why it matters to you: good writing only works if it actually reaches the inbox. The Mailer handles the technical craft of deliverability so your approved emails are seen — without you thinking about any of it.

👀

The Inbox Watcher · the payoff

Catches the warm replies

Turns automated sending into "a real lead is waiting for you."

Runs  All day, every ~20 min It watches  Your outreach inbox It surfaces  Genuine human replies

This is the teammate that closes the loop — and the reason the whole system exists. Throughout the day it keeps checking the outreach inbox and reads every response, sorting it into one of three buckets:

  • A real human reply — a warm lead. It flags this for you right away so you can jump in personally.
  • An auto-reply ("out of office") — handled automatically, never bothers you.
  • A bounce (dead address) — cleaned up quietly in the background.

When a genuine reply comes in, the lead is flipped to "Responded — Owner Follow-up" — your cue to take over the conversation.

Why it matters to you: you never have to sit and watch an inbox. Warm leads surface fast and land in front of you — and your time only ever goes to people who actually wrote back.

🔁

The Follow-Up · the long game

Revives the quiet ones

Keeps good leads from ever going to waste.

Runs  Weekly (Sunday mornings) It revisits  Good leads who never replied It does  A fresh attempt, months later

Not everyone replies the first time — and that's normal. Leads who got the full sequence but stayed quiet aren't thrown away. After a 90-day cool-off, the Follow-Up quietly pulls the good-quality ones back into the pipeline for a fresh attempt.

Crucially, it starts clean: it clears the old emails and writes a brand-new approach that never references the earlier outreach, so it feels like a genuine first hello. It caps this at two retries, and always respects anyone who's asked not to be contacted again.

Why it matters to you: timing is everything in sales — a "no reply" often just means "not right now." This teammate gives your best prospects a second, well-timed shot, so good leads keep working for you long after the first email.
