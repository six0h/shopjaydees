Google Cloud — Access Setup

Grant Cody full admin access

Two quick tasks in the Google Cloud console. This gives Cody everything he needs to finish the build and run it for you — no passwords shared. Takes about five minutes.

Task 1 of 2

Add Cody at the organization level

Sign in with your ShopJaydees owner account, then:

1

Go to console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/iam

2

At the very top of the page, click the resource picker and switch it from a project to your Organization (shopjaydees.com) — not a single project.

3

Click Grant Access.

4

New principals: cody@sixohquad.com

5

Add both of these roles, then click Save:

Organization AdministratorFull control over the organization and its projects.
Organization Policy AdministratorLets Cody clear the security policy that's currently blocking the deploy.

Can't switch to the Organization? You'll need to be signed in as the account that owns shopjaydees.com. If it's greyed out, send Cody a screenshot and he'll point you to the right step.

Task 2 of 2

Add Cody to billing

This lets Cody watch costs, set budget alerts, and sort out any billing hiccups. Your card details stay private to you.

1

Go to console.cloud.google.com/billing and open your billing account.

2

In the left menu, click Account management.

3

Click Add principal and enter cody@sixohquad.com.

4

Role: Billing Account Administrator → Save.

That's everything

Send Cody a quick note when done

Once both tasks are saved, email Cody and he takes it from there. Stuck on any screen? Snap a screenshot and send it over.
