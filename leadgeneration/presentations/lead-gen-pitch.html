04 / 17
Stage 1

Discover

An AI agent searches Google Maps every week for businesses, schools, and teams in your target geography.

  • 50-150 raw prospects per week
  • Deduplicates against existing contacts
  • Categorized by segment automatically
Stage 2

Enrich

Each prospect gets researched daily:

  • Decision-maker name & email
  • Company size & branding quality
  • Recent news & social activity
  • Fit score from 1-5
05 / 17
Stage 3

Personalize

AI generates a 3-touch email sequence plus a LinkedIn connection note — tailored to each prospect.

  • References their business by name
  • Connects to their industry & season
  • Mentions Wear It Forward where relevant
Stage 4

Outreach

You review, approve, and the system sends:

  • Email delivered via cold email tool
  • LinkedIn messages drafted for you
  • Opens & replies tracked automatically
  • Warm leads flagged for your follow-up
