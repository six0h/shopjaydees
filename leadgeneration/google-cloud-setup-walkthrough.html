Part 1 of 4

Create your Google Cloud account.

Google Cloud is where the pipeline's automation will live. Signing in for the first time creates your account automatically.

console.cloud.google.com
1
Open console.cloud.google.com in your browser
2
Sign in with your ShopJaydees Google account
3
Choose Canada as your country and agree to the Terms of Service
4
You'll land on the Console dashboard — that's it, account created
💡 If Google offers a free trial with $300 in credits, take it! It covers the first months of pipeline costs entirely.
04 / 12
Part 1 of 4 continued

Create a project.

A "project" is just a labelled box that holds everything — the agents, the AI, the billing. We only need one.

1
In the top bar, click the project picker (it may say "Select a project")
2
Click New Project in the window that pops up
3
Name it ShopJaydees Lead Gen
4
Click Create, then select the new project so its name shows in the top bar
05 / 12
Part 2 of 4

Set up billing.

This is how Google charges the small monthly usage fees. You'll always see every charge, and Cody sets up budget alerts so there are no surprises.

console.cloud.google.com/billing
1
Go to console.cloud.google.com/billing
2
Click Create account (or Add billing account)
3
Set country to Canada, fill in the business details
4
Add your credit card and submit
🔒 Google may place a tiny temporary charge (around $1) to verify the card — it disappears on its own.
06 / 12
Part 2 of 4 continued

Link billing to your project.

One more click so the ShopJaydees Lead Gen project knows which card to bill.

1
On the Billing page, open the My Projects tab
2
Find ShopJaydees Lead Gen in the list
3
Click the ⋮ three-dot menu beside it → Change billing
4
Pick the billing account you just created → Set account
💰 Expected costs: $2-5/mo for Gemini AI + $0-5/mo for hosting. That's the whole Google bill.
07 / 12
Part 3 of 4

Set up Google AI Studio.

AI Studio is the doorway to Gemini — the AI that researches leads and writes your personalized messages. You just need to switch it on.

aistudio.google.com
1
Open aistudio.google.com and sign in with the same Google account
2
Accept the terms when prompted
3
Click Get API key → Create API key
4
When asked for a project, choose ShopJaydees Lead Gen
🔐 The key is like a password — don't email it or screenshot it. You don't need to save it anywhere; Cody will manage keys securely once he has access.
08 / 12
Part 4 of 4

Invite Cody as admin.

Granting Cody the Owner role — Google Cloud's full-admin access — lets him build and run everything without ever needing your password.

console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin
1
Check the top bar shows ShopJaydees Lead Gen, then go to console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin
2
Click Grant Access near the top of the page
3
New principals: cody@sixohquad.com
4
Role: choose Basic → Owner, then click Save
09 / 12
Part 4 of 4 continued

And billing access too.

This lets Cody watch the costs, set budget alerts, and sort out any billing hiccups — your card details stay private to you.

1
Go back to console.cloud.google.com/billing and open your billing account
2
In the left menu, click Account management
3
Click Add principal (right-hand panel), enter cody@sixohquad.com
4
Role: Billing Account Administrator → Save
