Revenue attribution — AI-sourced

No AI-sourced opportunities were logged in your CRM this month, and that is expected this early. Custom-apparel sales cycles run roughly six to ten weeks from first contact to delivered goods, so revenue trails outreach by weeks or months. As replies turn into quotes, tagging each one Lead Source = AI Outreach in your CRM is what makes it show up here in future reports.

What is working, and what we tuned

July was about making the outreach unmistakably yours, based on your feedback:

  • Rebranded to Jaydees Apparel. The outreach now introduces the business by its proper name throughout.
  • Writing that sounds like a person, not a machine. We tightened the copy so every email reads as a genuine note from Ellie, with the AI tells removed.
  • On-brand and on-season. Messaging is locked to a no-obligation quote — no invented products — and always speaks to the correct upcoming season, so July sells fall.
  • Running on a schedule. The system finds prospects, writes, and queues each batch automatically. Your prospecting requests steer where it looks, and your approvals decide what goes out.
