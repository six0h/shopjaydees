Behind the Scenes

The system
takes over.

After you create a request, the Discovery Agent handles everything automatically. You don't need to touch Hunter.io or score leads yourself.

  • Finds matching companies automatically
  • Gets decision-maker contact info
  • Scores each lead (1-5) based on fit
  • High-scoring leads enter the personalization pipeline
  • Low-scoring leads get parked for later

Warm Leads

When someone
replies.

  • Respond personally — these are real conversations
  • Reference their specific situation from the task details
  • Mention Wear It Forward if it fits naturally
  • Move to "Won" when they become a customer

Your Routine

A rhythm
you can keep.

Check Approval Queue — review and approve drafts
Daily

Daily (~5 min)
Send LinkedIn messages from the queue
Weekly (~10 min)
Create 1-2 new Prospecting Requests to keep the pipeline full
Monthly (30 min)
Review call with Cody — results, targeting adjustments, seasonal planning

Handled for You

What you don't
need to think about.

The system handles all of this automatically. You never need to touch these processes or tools directly.

  • Finding companies (Discovery Agent + Hunter.io)
  • Scoring leads on fit and quality
  • Writing personalized draft messages
  • Sending email sequences (3 touches over 9 days)
  • Tracking opens, clicks, and replies
  • Moving leads through pipeline stages
  • Handling bounces and unsubscribes
  • 90-day dormancy cool-off and re-engagement
  • Deduplicating leads across searches
